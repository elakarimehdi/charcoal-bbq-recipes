Argentine steakhouse

Whole picanha with smoked chimichurri; Smoked paprika romesco; Grilled calçots; Ember-roasted beetroot with goat cheese and orange. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 75 min · Planning total: 2 h 30 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Optional: make sauces 1 day ahead. Keep goat cheese chilled until serving.

Serving schedule

150 min before serving: light charcoal and prepare sauces. 110 min before: start beetroot. 90 min before: start picanha indirectly. Sear when its centre reaches 55–58°C. Grill calçots while beef rests and dress beetroot. The agreed menu combines Brazilian and Catalan dishes.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Whole picanha with smoked chimichurri

  • 1200 g whole picanha with fat cap
  • 12 g salt
  • 3 g black pepper
  • 30 g parsley leaves
  • 10 g fresh oregano leaves
  • 8 g garlic
  • 30 g red wine vinegar
  • 70 g olive oil
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 1 g chilli flakes
  • 2 g salt for sauce

Smoked paprika romesco

  • 200 g drained roasted red peppers
  • 100 g ripe tomato, quartered
  • 60 g toasted almonds
  • 5 g garlic
  • 20 g sherry vinegar
  • 50 g olive oil
  • 3 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g salt

Grilled calçots

  • 600 g calçots or large spring onions
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 2 g salt

Ember-roasted beetroot with goat cheese and orange

  • 600 g small beetroot (4)
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 2 g salt
  • 100 g soft goat cheese
  • 200 g peeled orange segments
  • 20 g shelled pistachios
  • 10 g red wine vinegar

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Whole picanha with smoked chimichurri

  1. TM7. Chop parsley, oregano and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add vinegar, oil, smoked paprika, chilli and 2 g sauce salt. Mix 10 sec / reverse / speed 2. Transfer.
  2. Outdoor grill. Trim fat cap to about 8 mm and score fat without cutting meat. Season beef with 12 g salt and pepper. Cook over indirect heat at 140–160°C, lid closed, for about 45–70 min until the centre reaches 55–58°C. Raise heat for searing; turn every 1–2 min over direct heat, moving away from flare-ups, until the centre reaches at least 63°C. Rest 10 min, then slice across the grain and serve with chimichurri.

Smoked paprika romesco

  1. TM7. Chop almonds and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Add peppers, tomato, vinegar, oil, paprika and salt. Blend 30 sec / speed 6. Scrape down and blend 10 sec / speed 6. Transfer and refrigerate; stir before serving.

Grilled calçots

  1. Outdoor grill. Trim roots and loose outer leaves. Toss onions with oil and salt. Grill over strong direct heat for 8–15 min, turning until outer layers char and centres soften. Wrap in clean foil for 10 min. Peel away blackened layers and serve with romesco.

Ember-roasted beetroot with goat cheese and orange

  1. Outdoor grill. Scrub beetroot, coat with olive oil and salt, and wrap each in a double layer of foil. Nestle at the edge of the ember bed, away from fierce coals. Turn every 20 min and cook 60–90 min until a skewer enters easily. Use heatproof gloves and tongs.
  2. Kitchen. Let beetroot cool enough to handle. Peel, cut into wedges and toss with vinegar. Top with goat cheese, orange and pistachios.
