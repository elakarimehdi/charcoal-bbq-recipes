American smokehouse

Rosemary and garlic beef brisket; Coffee and black pepper beef short ribs; Tahini sauce; Charred pointed cabbage; Hasselback sweet potatoes with whipped feta. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 150 min · Planning total: 39 h 00 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Required: season beef 12–24 h ahead. Prepare sauces. Total time allows 24 h dry seasoning plus 15 h on the day; reserve another 2 h if cuts cook slowly. This generous four-person menu makes substantial leftovers.

Serving schedule

15 h before serving: light charcoal and start brisket. 11 h before: add ribs. Wrap each cut when its bark holds. Finish each cut by tenderness, then hold safely above 60°C. About 90 min before serving, move finished beef to a warm oven and increase grill heat to cook vegetables. Use a 120°C oven to finish wrapped beef if grill space or heat control requires it.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Rosemary and garlic beef brisket

  • 2500 g beef brisket flat with 6 mm fat cap
  • 25 g salt
  • 15 g coarse black pepper
  • 8 g garlic powder
  • 5 g dried rosemary
  • 150 g hot water for wrapping

Coffee and black pepper beef short ribs

  • 1800 g thick bone-in beef short ribs
  • 18 g salt
  • 12 g coarse black pepper
  • 8 g finely ground coffee
  • 8 g brown sugar
  • 5 g garlic powder
  • 150 g hot water for wrapping

Tahini sauce

  • 80 g tahini
  • 40 g lemon juice
  • 60 g water
  • 4 g garlic
  • 2 g salt

Charred pointed cabbage

  • 700 g pointed cabbage (1)
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 3 g salt
  • 15 g lemon juice

Hasselback sweet potatoes with whipped feta

  • 800 g sweet potatoes (4 small)
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 3 g salt
  • 150 g feta
  • 100 g Greek yoghurt
  • 15 g lemon juice

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Rosemary and garlic beef brisket

  1. TM7, day before. Grind rosemary 10 sec / speed 8 in a dry bowl. Add salt, pepper and garlic powder; mix 10 sec / speed 3. Rub over brisket and refrigerate, covered, 12–24 h.
  2. Outdoor grill. Bank lit charcoal to one side of the Bison and place a drip pan under the empty zone. Stabilise grate-level temperature at 110–125°C. Put brisket above the empty zone, thick end towards the heat. Add one small food-grade oak wood chunk if desired. Close lid; check heat and fuel every 30–45 min. Replenish with lit charcoal as needed.
  3. Outdoor grill. Smoke for about 5–7 h until bark holds and the centre reaches roughly 70–75°C. Wrap tightly in two layers of foil with 150 g hot water. Return to indirect heat for about 3–6 h until the thickest part reaches 93–96°C and a probe enters with little resistance. Tenderness determines completion; allow a 2 h buffer.
  4. Kitchen. Rest wrapped brisket for 1–2 h in a warm oven, monitoring meat to keep it at least 60°C. Slice across the grain just before serving. This menu makes leftovers; chill extra portions promptly.

Coffee and black pepper beef short ribs

  1. TM7, day before. Mix salt, pepper, coffee, sugar and garlic powder 10 sec / speed 3 in a dry bowl. Rub over ribs and refrigerate, covered, overnight.
  2. Outdoor grill. Cook ribs bone-side down beside brisket over indirect heat at 110–125°C. Allow 7–10 h. At about 70–75°C internal temperature, once bark holds, wrap in foil with 150 g hot water. Continue until meat reaches roughly 93–96°C and a probe enters easily. Rest wrapped for 45–60 min, keeping meat above 60°C if holding longer.

Tahini sauce

  1. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add tahini, lemon juice, water and salt. Mix 30 sec / speed 4. Transfer and refrigerate.

Charred pointed cabbage

  1. Outdoor grill. Cut cabbage into 4 wedges, keeping the core attached. Brush with oil and salt. Grill cut sides over medium direct heat for 4–5 min each. Move to indirect heat at 180–200°C for 10–20 min until the core softens. Finish with lemon juice and tahini sauce.

Hasselback sweet potatoes with whipped feta

  1. TM7. Blend feta, yoghurt and lemon juice 30 sec / speed 4. Scrape down and mix 15 sec / speed 4. Transfer and refrigerate.
  2. Outdoor grill. Cut sweet potatoes at 5 mm intervals without cutting through the bases. Rub oil and salt into cuts. Cook indirectly at 180–200°C for 60–80 min until tender. After the beef finishes, increase the grill temperature for the vegetables. Serve potatoes with whipped feta.
