Spanish coastal barbecue

Charcoal octopus; Smoked paprika romesco; Giant prawns with miso browned butter; Tahini sauce; Grilled calçots; Charred pointed cabbage. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 100 min · Planning total: 4 h 00 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified successful Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Required for this schedule: simmer octopus 1 day ahead, cool promptly and refrigerate. Make romesco and tahini ahead. Total time covers 90 min advance cooking plus 150 min on the day, excluding overnight chilling.

Serving schedule

150 min before serving: prepare sauces and prawns. 60 min before: light charcoal. 35 min before: start cabbage and calçots. Grill octopus for the starter, then grill prawns immediately before the main course. Serve each batch hot.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Charcoal octopus

  • 1200 g cleaned raw octopus, thawed if frozen
  • 2000 g water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 15 g lemon juice

Smoked paprika romesco

  • 200 g drained roasted red peppers
  • 100 g ripe tomato, quartered
  • 60 g toasted almonds
  • 5 g garlic
  • 20 g sherry vinegar
  • 50 g olive oil
  • 3 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g salt

Giant prawns with miso browned butter

  • 1200 g raw whole large prawns
  • 70 g unsalted butter
  • 25 g white miso
  • 15 g lemon juice
  • 5 g garlic
  • 10 g neutral oil

Tahini sauce

  • 80 g tahini
  • 40 g lemon juice
  • 60 g water
  • 4 g garlic
  • 2 g salt

Grilled calçots

  • 600 g calçots or large spring onions
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 2 g salt

Charred pointed cabbage

  • 700 g pointed cabbage (1)
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 3 g salt
  • 15 g lemon juice

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Charcoal octopus

  1. Hob, day before. Bring water and bay leaves to a gentle simmer in a large saucepan. Add octopus, cover and simmer gently for 60–90 min until a knife enters the thickest tentacle easily; larger animals may need longer. Drain, cut into portions and cool promptly in shallow containers. Refrigerate within 2 h, or within 1 h in ambient heat above 32°C. Use the next day.
  2. Outdoor grill. Pat octopus dry and coat with oil. Grill over medium-high direct heat for 3–4 min per side to char. Move thick pieces to indirect heat until the centre reaches 74°C when reheating chilled cooked octopus. Finish with paprika and lemon juice; serve with romesco.

Smoked paprika romesco

  1. TM7. Chop almonds and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Add peppers, tomato, vinegar, oil, paprika and salt. Blend 30 sec / speed 6. Scrape down and blend 10 sec / speed 6. Transfer and refrigerate; stir before serving.

Giant prawns with miso browned butter

  1. Hob. Melt butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Stir for 3–5 min until milk solids turn golden and smell nutty. Remove from heat immediately and cool for 5 min.
  2. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7 and scrape down. Add warm browned butter, miso and lemon juice. Mix 20 sec / speed 3. Divide into basting and serving portions.
  3. Outdoor grill. Split prawns through their backs, keep shells on and remove veins. Brush with oil. Grill shell-side down over medium-high heat for 3–5 min, then flesh-side down for 1–2 min. Brush with basting butter near the end and check the thickest flesh reaches 63°C. Discard basting leftovers and serve with clean butter.

Tahini sauce

  1. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add tahini, lemon juice, water and salt. Mix 30 sec / speed 4. Transfer and refrigerate.

Grilled calçots

  1. Outdoor grill. Trim roots and loose outer leaves. Toss onions with oil and salt. Grill over strong direct heat for 8–15 min, turning until outer layers char and centres soften. Wrap in clean foil for 10 min. Peel away blackened layers and serve with romesco.

Charred pointed cabbage

  1. Outdoor grill. Cut cabbage into 4 wedges, keeping the core attached. Brush with oil and salt. Grill cut sides over medium direct heat for 4–5 min each. Move to indirect heat at 180–200°C for 10–20 min until the core softens. Finish with lemon juice and tahini sauce.
