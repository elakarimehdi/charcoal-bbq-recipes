North African lamb feast

Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder; Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh; Tahini sauce; Whole smoked cauliflower. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 100 min · Planning total: 31 h 00 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified successful Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Recommended: marinate lamb 12–24 h. Make glaze, labneh and tahini 1 day ahead. Total time allows 24 h marinating plus 7 h cooking day.

Serving schedule

7 h before serving: light charcoal and start lamb. 100 min before: add cauliflower. 50 min before: steam carrots. Rest lamb for 30 min while you grill carrots and finish cauliflower. Allow extra time if lamb remains tough.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder

  • 2200 g bone-in lamb shoulder
  • 20 g garlic
  • 30 g olive oil
  • 8 g ground cumin
  • 6 g ground coriander
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 18 g salt
  • 3 g black pepper
  • 250 g water for roasting tray
  • 90 g pomegranate molasses
  • 35 g honey
  • 25 g lemon juice
  • 30 g water for glaze

Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh

  • 600 g carrots, peeled and halved lengthwise
  • 1000 g water for steaming
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 25 g honey
  • 20 g harissa paste
  • 15 g lemon juice
  • 2 g salt
  • 200 g labneh
  • 20 g Greek yoghurt

Tahini sauce

  • 80 g tahini
  • 40 g lemon juice
  • 60 g water
  • 4 g garlic
  • 2 g salt

Whole smoked cauliflower

  • 900 g cauliflower (1 head)
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 4 g ground cumin
  • 3 g smoked paprika
  • 4 g salt
  • 60 g pomegranate seeds
  • 25 g shelled pistachios

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder

  1. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add olive oil, cumin, coriander, paprika, salt and pepper; mix 15 sec / speed 4. Rub over lamb, cover and refrigerate 12–24 h.
  2. TM7. In a clean bowl, mix pomegranate molasses, honey, lemon juice and 30 g water 15 sec / speed 3. Cook 5 min / 90°C / speed 2. Transfer to a clean container.
  3. Outdoor grill. Set up indirect heat at 140–160°C. Place lamb in a roasting tray with 250 g water above the empty charcoal zone. Cook with lid closed for 3 h. Cover tray tightly with foil and cook another 2–3 h until the thickest meat reaches about 93–96°C and a probe slides in easily; allow longer if tough. Add small amounts of hot water if the tray dries.
  4. Outdoor grill. Uncover, brush with glaze and cook indirectly for 15–20 min until sticky. Rest under loose foil for 30 min. Pull the meat and spoon over tray juices after removing excess fat.

Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh

  1. TM7. Mix labneh and yoghurt 20 sec / speed 3. Transfer and refrigerate. Mix oil, honey, harissa, lemon juice and salt 15 sec / speed 4; transfer.
  2. TM7. Add 1000 g water to the clean bowl. Arrange carrots in Varoma with steam gaps. Fit Varoma and its lid, without bowl lid cap. Steam 20–25 min / Varoma / speed 1 until just tender. Check water remains if extending steaming.
  3. Outdoor grill. Toss carrots with harissa glaze. Grill in a basket over medium direct heat for 4–6 min, turning frequently. Spread labneh on a platter and top with hot carrots.

Tahini sauce

  1. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add tahini, lemon juice, water and salt. Mix 30 sec / speed 4. Transfer and refrigerate.

Whole smoked cauliflower

  1. Outdoor grill. Trim cauliflower leaves and core base while keeping florets attached. Rub with oil, cumin, paprika and salt. Put in a small covered roasting dish beside the lamb for 75–100 min at 150–170°C, until a knife enters the core easily. Uncover for the final 15 min. If the lamb needs a lower heat, allow longer. Serve with tahini, pomegranate seeds and pistachios.
