Mediterranean seafood

Butterfly sea bass with preserved lemon chermoula; Artichokes with preserved lemon aioli; Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 55 min · Planning total: 1 h 40 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Optional: make chermoula and aioli 1 day ahead. Trim and simmer artichokes on the day.

Serving schedule

100 min before serving: make sauces and simmer artichokes. 45 min before: light charcoal. 20 min before: grill fish and artichokes. Grill peaches after the main course.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Butterfly sea bass with preserved lemon chermoula

  • 1200 g whole sea bass, 2 fish, scaled, gutted and butterflied by fishmonger
  • 25 g coriander leaves and tender stems
  • 20 g parsley leaves
  • 8 g garlic
  • 30 g preserved lemon rind, rinsed
  • 30 g lemon juice
  • 60 g olive oil
  • 4 g ground cumin
  • 3 g sweet paprika
  • 2 g salt

Artichokes with preserved lemon aioli

  • 800 g small globe artichokes (4)
  • 1500 g water for simmering
  • 30 g lemon juice for simmering
  • 20 g olive oil
  • 2 g salt
  • 120 g commercial mayonnaise made with pasteurised egg
  • 15 g preserved lemon rind, rinsed
  • 4 g garlic
  • 10 g lemon juice for aioli

Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey

  • 600 g firm ripe peaches (4)
  • 200 g mascarpone
  • 60 g Greek yoghurt
  • 15 g icing sugar
  • 60 g honey
  • 1 g fresh thyme leaves
  • 10 g neutral oil
  • 20 g shelled pistachios

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Butterfly sea bass with preserved lemon chermoula

  1. TM7. Chop herbs, garlic and preserved lemon 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add lemon juice, olive oil, cumin, paprika and salt. Mix 15 sec / speed 4. Reserve half in a clean bowl for serving.
  2. Kitchen. Pat fish dry. Spread remaining chermoula over flesh 15–20 min before grilling; keep fish chilled until the grill is ready.
  3. Outdoor grill. Use a clean, oiled fish basket. Grill fish over medium direct heat, skin towards the coals first, for about 6–8 min, then turn and cook 4–6 min. Move to indirect heat if necessary. Check the thickest flesh reaches 63°C. Lift out carefully and serve with reserved chermoula.

Artichokes with preserved lemon aioli

  1. TM7. Chop preserved lemon and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add mayonnaise and 10 g lemon juice. Mix 15 sec / speed 3. Transfer and refrigerate.
  2. Hob. Trim artichoke stems and tough leaves; halve and remove fuzzy chokes. Put in a saucepan with 1500 g water and 30 g lemon juice. Cover and simmer 20–30 min until a knife enters the bases easily. Drain well.
  3. Outdoor grill. Brush artichokes with olive oil and salt. Grill cut-side down over medium direct heat for 4–6 min, then turn for 2 min. Serve with cold aioli.

Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey

  1. TM7. Chop pistachios 3 sec / speed 5 and set aside. Warm honey and thyme 3 min / 50°C / speed 1; transfer. Cool and clean bowl. Mix cold mascarpone, yoghurt and icing sugar 15 sec / speed 3. Scrape down and stir with spatula if needed; refrigerate.
  2. Outdoor grill. Halve and stone peaches. Brush cut faces with oil and grill over medium direct heat for 3–4 min. Turn and cook 2–3 min until tender. Serve with mascarpone, thyme honey and pistachios.
