Korean charcoal barbecue

Korean galbi; Miso aubergine; Charred broccolini with almond gremolata. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 55 min · Planning total: 13 h 30 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified successful Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Required: marinate ribs 6–12 h in the refrigerator. Make miso glaze ahead. Total time includes 12 h marinating and 90 min on the day.

Serving schedule

90 min before serving: light charcoal and make sides. 50 min before: start aubergines. 15 min before: grill broccolini and ribs in separate grill areas. Rest ribs 3 min and finish aubergine glaze.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Korean galbi

  • 1200 g flanken-cut beef short ribs, 5–8 mm thick
  • 150 g peeled cored pear
  • 80 g soy sauce
  • 30 g brown sugar
  • 20 g rice vinegar
  • 15 g sesame oil
  • 15 g garlic
  • 10 g peeled ginger
  • 40 g spring onions

Miso aubergine

  • 600 g aubergines (2 medium)
  • 50 g white miso
  • 25 g honey
  • 20 g water
  • 15 g rice vinegar
  • 10 g sesame oil
  • 15 g neutral oil

Charred broccolini with almond gremolata

  • 500 g broccolini
  • 40 g toasted almonds
  • 15 g parsley leaves
  • 4 g garlic
  • 2 g finely grated lemon zest
  • 20 g lemon juice
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 3 g salt

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Korean galbi

  1. TM7. Blend pear, soy sauce, sugar, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger and spring onions 20 sec / speed 7. Scrape down and blend 10 sec / speed 7.
  2. Kitchen. Pat ribs dry and remove loose bone fragments with kitchen paper. Coat with marinade in a covered container. Refrigerate 6–12 h. Drain well before grilling and discard marinade.
  3. Outdoor grill. Grill ribs over medium-high direct heat for about 3–4 min per side, turning sooner if sugar darkens. Keep a clear zone for flare-ups. Check the meat reaches 63°C, away from bone, and rest 3 min before serving.

Miso aubergine

  1. TM7. Mix miso, honey, water, rice vinegar and sesame oil 15 sec / speed 4. Transfer to a clean container.
  2. Outdoor grill. Halve aubergines lengthwise and score the flesh without piercing the skin. Brush with neutral oil. Cook cut-side up over indirect heat at 190–210°C, lid closed, for 30–45 min until fully soft. Brush with all the glaze. Finish over moderate direct heat for 2–3 min; turn carefully and move away from coals if the glaze darkens quickly.

Charred broccolini with almond gremolata

  1. TM7. Chop almonds, parsley, garlic and lemon zest 4 sec / speed 6. Transfer to a bowl and stir in lemon juice and 10 g olive oil.
  2. Outdoor grill. Toss broccolini with remaining 15 g olive oil and salt. Grill in a basket over medium direct heat for 6–10 min, turning often, until stalks soften. Spoon gremolata over the vegetables.
