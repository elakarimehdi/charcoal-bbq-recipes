Chef's charcoal tasting menu

King oyster mushrooms with miso butter; Charcoal octopus; Smoked paprika romesco; Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder; Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh; Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 180 min · Planning total: 32 h 30 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified successful Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Two days ahead: order and trim meat. One day ahead: marinate lamb, simmer octopus and make sauces. Total time includes 24 h marinating, 90 min advance octopus cooking and 7 h on the day. Serve small first and second courses; keep extra lamb for another meal.

Serving schedule

7 h before the main course: light charcoal and start lamb. 60 min before: prepare mushrooms and steam carrots. Serve mushrooms first, then octopus with romesco 15 min later. Keep lamb above 60°C if ready early. Glaze carrots and serve lamb after its 30 min rest. Grill peaches for dessert.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

King oyster mushrooms with miso butter

  • 400 g king oyster mushrooms
  • 50 g softened unsalted butter
  • 25 g white miso
  • 10 g lemon juice
  • 10 g neutral oil

Charcoal octopus

  • 1200 g cleaned raw octopus, thawed if frozen
  • 2000 g water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 15 g olive oil
  • 2 g smoked paprika
  • 15 g lemon juice

Smoked paprika romesco

  • 200 g drained roasted red peppers
  • 100 g ripe tomato, quartered
  • 60 g toasted almonds
  • 5 g garlic
  • 20 g sherry vinegar
  • 50 g olive oil
  • 3 g smoked paprika
  • 2 g salt

Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder

  • 2200 g bone-in lamb shoulder
  • 20 g garlic
  • 30 g olive oil
  • 8 g ground cumin
  • 6 g ground coriander
  • 4 g smoked paprika
  • 18 g salt
  • 3 g black pepper
  • 250 g water for roasting tray
  • 90 g pomegranate molasses
  • 35 g honey
  • 25 g lemon juice
  • 30 g water for glaze

Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh

  • 600 g carrots, peeled and halved lengthwise
  • 1000 g water for steaming
  • 25 g olive oil
  • 25 g honey
  • 20 g harissa paste
  • 15 g lemon juice
  • 2 g salt
  • 200 g labneh
  • 20 g Greek yoghurt

Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey

  • 600 g firm ripe peaches (4)
  • 200 g mascarpone
  • 60 g Greek yoghurt
  • 15 g icing sugar
  • 60 g honey
  • 1 g fresh thyme leaves
  • 10 g neutral oil
  • 20 g shelled pistachios

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

King oyster mushrooms with miso butter

  1. TM7. Mix butter, miso and lemon juice 20 sec / speed 4. Scrape down and mix another 10 sec / speed 4 if needed.
  2. Outdoor grill. Cut mushrooms into 2 cm rounds and score both faces. Brush with oil. Grill over moderate direct heat for 4–6 min per side until tender. Brush with miso butter during the final minute. Serve immediately.

Charcoal octopus

  1. Hob, day before. Bring water and bay leaves to a gentle simmer in a large saucepan. Add octopus, cover and simmer gently for 60–90 min until a knife enters the thickest tentacle easily; larger animals may need longer. Drain, cut into portions and cool promptly in shallow containers. Refrigerate within 2 h, or within 1 h in ambient heat above 32°C. Use the next day.
  2. Outdoor grill. Pat octopus dry and coat with oil. Grill over medium-high direct heat for 3–4 min per side to char. Move thick pieces to indirect heat until the centre reaches 74°C when reheating chilled cooked octopus. Finish with paprika and lemon juice; serve with romesco.

Smoked paprika romesco

  1. TM7. Chop almonds and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Add peppers, tomato, vinegar, oil, paprika and salt. Blend 30 sec / speed 6. Scrape down and blend 10 sec / speed 6. Transfer and refrigerate; stir before serving.

Pomegranate-glazed lamb shoulder

  1. TM7. Chop garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add olive oil, cumin, coriander, paprika, salt and pepper; mix 15 sec / speed 4. Rub over lamb, cover and refrigerate 12–24 h.
  2. TM7. In a clean bowl, mix pomegranate molasses, honey, lemon juice and 30 g water 15 sec / speed 3. Cook 5 min / 90°C / speed 2. Transfer to a clean container.
  3. Outdoor grill. Set up indirect heat at 140–160°C. Place lamb in a roasting tray with 250 g water above the empty charcoal zone. Cook with lid closed for 3 h. Cover tray tightly with foil and cook another 2–3 h until the thickest meat reaches about 93–96°C and a probe slides in easily; allow longer if tough. Add small amounts of hot water if the tray dries.
  4. Outdoor grill. Uncover, brush with glaze and cook indirectly for 15–20 min until sticky. Rest under loose foil for 30 min. Pull the meat and spoon over tray juices after removing excess fat.

Harissa honey carrots with whipped labneh

  1. TM7. Mix labneh and yoghurt 20 sec / speed 3. Transfer and refrigerate. Mix oil, honey, harissa, lemon juice and salt 15 sec / speed 4; transfer.
  2. TM7. Add 1000 g water to the clean bowl. Arrange carrots in Varoma with steam gaps. Fit Varoma and its lid, without bowl lid cap. Steam 20–25 min / Varoma / speed 1 until just tender. Check water remains if extending steaming.
  3. Outdoor grill. Toss carrots with harissa glaze. Grill in a basket over medium direct heat for 4–6 min, turning frequently. Spread labneh on a platter and top with hot carrots.

Grilled peaches with mascarpone and thyme honey

  1. TM7. Chop pistachios 3 sec / speed 5 and set aside. Warm honey and thyme 3 min / 50°C / speed 1; transfer. Cool and clean bowl. Mix cold mascarpone, yoghurt and icing sugar 15 sec / speed 3. Scrape down and stir with spatula if needed; refrigerate.
  2. Outdoor grill. Halve and stone peaches. Brush cut faces with oil and grill over medium direct heat for 3–4 min. Turn and cook 2–3 min until tender. Serve with mascarpone, thyme honey and pistachios.
