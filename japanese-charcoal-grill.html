Japanese charcoal grill

Yakitori chicken with tare; Miso aubergine; King oyster mushrooms with miso butter. Four servings. Thermomix TM7 preparation and Bull Bison charcoal cooking.

Active work: about 45 min · Planning total: 1 h 30 min, including listed advance work or marinating

Use the TM7 indoors. Fit its bowl lid and lid cap for mixing and heating, except when fitting the Varoma. Stop blades before scraping. Clean the bowl between preparations. These original recipes use proposed settings; we have not kitchen-tested them or verified Cookidoo import. Keep raw proteins and ready-to-eat food separate. Refrigerate perishable food at 4°C or below. Use clean serving utensils and a food thermometer. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 h, or 1 h when outdoor temperature exceeds 32°C.

Advance preparation

Optional: make sauces 1 day ahead. Keep chicken separate.

Serving schedule

90 min before serving: light charcoal and make sauces. 50 min before: start aubergines. 25 min before: start chicken. 15 min before: grill mushrooms. Finish glazes and serve together.

Bull Bison setup

Use the Bull Bison outdoors. Follow your model manual and charcoal loading limits. Light charcoal about 30–40 min before cooking. Keep one tray area free of coals for indirect cooking and a drip pan; bank coals in the adjacent area. Adjust charcoal-bed height and vents to control heat. Measure temperature at grate level. Keep the lid closed for indirect cooking. Move food to the empty zone during flare-ups. Add lit charcoal with heatproof tools during long cooks; never add lighter fluid to a lit grill.

Ingredients

Yakitori chicken with tare

  • 800 g boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 100 g spring onions, trimmed
  • 100 g soy sauce
  • 70 g water
  • 50 g brown sugar
  • 20 g rice vinegar
  • 15 g peeled ginger
  • 10 g peeled garlic
  • 10 g neutral oil
  • 5 g sesame seeds
  • 20 g spring onion greens

Miso aubergine

  • 600 g aubergines (2 medium)
  • 50 g white miso
  • 25 g honey
  • 20 g water
  • 15 g rice vinegar
  • 10 g sesame oil
  • 15 g neutral oil

King oyster mushrooms with miso butter

  • 400 g king oyster mushrooms
  • 50 g softened unsalted butter
  • 25 g white miso
  • 10 g lemon juice
  • 10 g neutral oil

Preparation and cooking

Follow the serving schedule above to overlap these component instructions.

Yakitori chicken with tare

  1. TM7. Chop ginger and garlic 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down. Add soy sauce, water, sugar and vinegar. Mix 10 sec / speed 4, then cook 15 min / 100°C / speed 2 with lid cap fitted. Transfer to a small saucepan and simmer on the hob for 3–8 min if the sauce still runs like water. Cool slightly; it thickens as it cools.
  2. Kitchen. Divide tare into separate basting and serving containers before handling chicken. Cut chicken into 3 cm pieces and spring onions into 3 cm lengths. Thread onto metal skewers and brush with oil.
  3. Outdoor grill. Grill skewers over moderate direct heat for 12–18 min, turning every 2–3 min. Brush with basting tare only during the last 3–4 min. Check several thick chicken pieces reach 74°C. Discard leftover basting sauce. Serve with clean tare, sesame seeds and spring onion greens.

Miso aubergine

  1. TM7. Mix miso, honey, water, rice vinegar and sesame oil 15 sec / speed 4. Transfer to a clean container.
  2. Outdoor grill. Halve aubergines lengthwise and score the flesh without piercing the skin. Brush with neutral oil. Cook cut-side up over indirect heat at 190–210°C, lid closed, for 30–45 min until fully soft. Brush with all the glaze. Finish over moderate direct heat for 2–3 min; turn carefully and move away from coals if the glaze darkens quickly.

King oyster mushrooms with miso butter

  1. TM7. Mix butter, miso and lemon juice 20 sec / speed 4. Scrape down and mix another 10 sec / speed 4 if needed.
  2. Outdoor grill. Cut mushrooms into 2 cm rounds and score both faces. Brush with oil. Grill over moderate direct heat for 4–6 min per side until tender. Brush with miso butter during the final minute. Serve immediately.
